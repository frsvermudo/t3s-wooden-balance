Greetings, Humanling.

We are the T³S, also known as the Archicarpangeneers, hailing from the planet of Brainrot, and we present ourselves before you this day as emissaries of our species, that we might proffer unto you an object most worthy of your gaze, inasmuch as your kind stands in need of novel contrivances derived from the observations we have made.

We have watched thee guess the weight of thy flour, thy fruit and thy fortunes, and we have found thy guessing wanting. Behold therefore the Balance: a weighing scale wrought wholly of timber, its beam, pillar and pans carved by our own three hands, that thy reckonings may at last be true.

We are three, the Architect, the Carpenter and the Engineer, and thus are we also called the Archicarpangeneers. Our patron is the Sahur, the drummer who knocks thrice upon thy door. Tung. Tung. Tung.

The name

T³S means T to the third power, S: three of us, like the three Tungs in Tung Tung Tung Sahur, the meme that inspired our name. Archicarpangeneers is our other name for ourselves, Architect + Carpenter + Engineer squashed into one word, one skill for each of us.

A good scale needs someone to design it, someone to build it and someone to check the math, so it took all three of us.

How the Balance works

A level beam rests on a single pivot. Whatever you set in one pan pulls that side down, and when both pans carry equal weight the beam sits level. Try it in the model above.

Our real one is made entirely of wood, so every joint, hook and hanger had to be fitted by hand.
